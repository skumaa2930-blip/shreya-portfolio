Adaptive Intervention Framework

How the system decides when to stay silent, when to inform, and when to intervene.

LOW RISK

Passive Logging

Reversible micro-transactions. Silent ledger reconciliations with no notification ping.

MEDIUM RISK

Glanceable Nudge

Elevated category burn rate. Ambient lock-screen badge when phone is next unlocked.

HIGH RISK

Mandated Consent

Discretionary capital transfer or subscription lock. Requires deliberate single tap.

CRITICAL RISK

Instant Halt

Immediate interrupt for rent threshold breach or suspected fraudulent debit.

Trust &
Transparency

Trust is the most critical part of a financial agent. It is built gradually through consistent, transparent behaviour not assumed upfront. If an agent can't say no, it wouldn't be an effective one.

Principle 01

User stays in control

Every automation retains a single zero-friction kill switch.

Principle 02

Clear financial context

Surfaces transparent deterministic formulas behind every decision.

Principle 03

Non-judgemental guidance

No shaming badges, red warnings, or guilt-tripping notifications.

Principle 04

Invisible reliability

Operates silently under the hood without asking for constant logging praise.

Principle 05

Automation builds gradually

Starts with pure observation before asking for autonomous execution keys.

Principle 06

Interrupts only when needed

Treats human focus as sacred, batching non-urgent context into calm recaps.
